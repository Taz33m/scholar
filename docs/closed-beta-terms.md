# Closed Beta Terms

This draft is a founder-friendly starting point for Apple Scholar's closed beta.
It is not legal advice and should be reviewed by a lawyer before broad rollout.

## Short Version

These terms are meant for:

- a small U.S. closed beta
- direct student users
- college-age users only
- no official LMS/gradebook integration yet

For the easiest path, keep the beta limited to users who are at least 18 years old.
